Our Story

A home for Nigerian football in Columbus.

Naija FC Columbus began with a simple idea: Nigerians in Central Ohio should have a place to play the game we grew up with, the way we grew up playing it, with pride, energy and respect.

Today the club brings together Nigerians from every state and background, along with friends who share our love of football. We play organized practice games, train together and show up for one another beyond the final whistle. What holds us together is character: how we treat each other, how we compete and how we represent Nigeria in the city we now call home.

We are one team. We come from many backgrounds. On this pitch, we are one Naija.

Our Values

Six standards every member carries.

Unity

We play as one Naija. State, tribe and language never divide the team.

Discipline

Punctuality, preparation and self-control, in training and in games.

Respect

Every teammate, opponent, referee and organizer is treated with dignity.

Safety

No dangerous play, no fighting and no threats. Everyone goes home well.

Integrity

Opportunities are earned on merit. Club decisions and money are handled honestly.

Community

We support one another and serve the wider Columbus community.

Our Foundation

Built on a written Constitution.

Naija FC Columbus operates under a formal Constitution, Rules & Regulations. It sets out how the club is run, how members are expected to behave and how every decision stays fair. Leadership and playing opportunities are based on character, commitment, competence, discipline, teamwork and service. Ethnicity, friendship and personal relationships play no part.

      Club Constitution

      Constitution, Rules & Regulations

      The written framework that governs Naija FC Columbus. These are its key provisions.

      This page summarizes the Constitution. Registered members receive the full document, and any question about it can be raised with club leadership.

      Article I

      Membership

      • Membership is open to people aged 18 and over.
      • Members agree to follow the Constitution, Rules & Regulations.
      • A registered member may invite a non-Nigerian friend aged 18+ to approved practice games, subject to club, safety, facility and organizer rules.
      Article II

      Leadership

      • Leadership roles are based on character, commitment, competence, discipline, teamwork and service.
      • Ethnicity, friendship and personal relationships are never grounds for appointment.
      Article III

      Anti-Tribalism

      • The club does not tolerate tribalism, ethnic discrimination or tribal insults of any kind.
      • Every member plays as one Naija, whatever their state, tribe or language.
      Article IV

      Conduct

      • Fighting, threats and harassment are prohibited on and off the pitch at club activities.
      • Members treat teammates, opponents, referees, organizers and facilities with respect.
      Article V

      Safety

      • Dangerous play is not tolerated.
      • Safety, facility rules and organizer decisions take priority at every session.
      Article VI

      Attendance & Check-in

      • For organized 11-a-side practice games, the first 22 eligible players to physically arrive and check in form the initial 22-player group.
      • This is subject to safety, eligibility, suspensions and organizer decisions.
      Article VII

      Discipline

      • Breaches of the Constitution are handled through the club's disciplinary process.
      • Suspended members may not take part in games while a suspension is in effect.
      Article VIII

      Financial Accountability

      • Club funds are handled transparently and accounted for to members.
      Article IX

      Fairness

      • Player opportunities are based on character, commitment, competence, discipline, teamwork and service.
      • Favoritism is not tolerated.
